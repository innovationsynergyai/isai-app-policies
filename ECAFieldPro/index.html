App-specific data practices (ECAFieldPro)

  • This app is a field-service tool for East Coast Air technicians. It stores job records on your device and syncs them with the company's Supabase database.
  • Information handled: customer names, email addresses, phone numbers and job-site addresses; precise location (for the corrosion log and local weather); photos taken for jobs; job notes, signatures and proposals; and proposal pricing and financing terms.
  • Third-party services used for app functionality: Supabase (data storage), GoHighLevel (CRM sync), OpenRouter (AI assistant requests, which may include your question and a job photo) and Open-Meteo (weather, which receives approximate coordinates).
  • The app does not use advertising or cross-app tracking and does not record audio.

App Store privacy summary: Contact information, precise location, photos, other user content and other financial information are collected, linked to the user, for app functionality only.

Your choices and deletion

You can delete the data held in the app by deleting the app from your device. To ask us to delete or export any information we hold about you, contact michael@innovationsynergyai.com. We respond within 30 days.
