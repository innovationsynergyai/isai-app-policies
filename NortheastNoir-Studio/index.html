App-specific data practices (Northeast Noir Studio)

  • Northeast Noir Studio is a creative studio tool. Your projects and settings are stored on your device; any API keys you enter are stored in the iOS Keychain.
  • AI generation: when you generate content, the text you enter is sent from your device to OpenRouter (openrouter.ai) using your own API key, which processes it under its own privacy terms. Optional GoHighLevel features send requests to services.leadconnectorhq.com with credentials you provide.
  • Microphone and speech recognition are used only if you allow them, to turn your voice into text. Face ID is used only to unlock the app and is handled by iOS; we never receive biometric data.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

App Store privacy summary: We do not collect data from this app; text you submit for generation is sent to the third-party service you configure.

Your choices and deletion

You can delete the data held in the app by deleting the app from your device. To ask us to delete or export any information we hold about you, contact michael@innovationsynergyai.com. We respond within 30 days.
